Liki Canonical Brand Definition (v3.1)

Brand（品牌）

中文

English

Liki

Liki is the brand.

Category（产品类别）

中文

命理师的 Skill

Liki 是一种面向中华命理的专业 Skill，而不是传统软件或聊天机器人。

English

Professional Skill for Chinese Metaphysics

Liki is a professional Skill designed for Chinese Metaphysics rather than a traditional software application or chatbot.

Positioning（品牌定位）

中文

«Liki，命理师的 Skill。»

这里的"命理师"代表专业标准，而不是用户边界。

Liki 按照命理师真实工作标准构建，因此专业人士能够信赖，普通用户同样可以自然使用。

English

«Professional Skill for Chinese Metaphysics»

Built to professional standards for Chinese Metaphysics, enabling professionals to work more efficiently while remaining accessible to everyone.

中文

- 专业（Professional）

English

Product Philosophy（产品理念）

中文

«把重复的工作交给 Liki，把专业判断留给命理师。»

Liki 负责处理重复、繁琐、可系统化的工作，例如：

- 排盘
- 真太阳时计算
- 历法计算
- 规则执行
- 资料整理
- 辅助分析
- 报告生成

命理师负责：

- 专业判断
- 经验积累
- 与客户沟通
- 最终决策

English

«Let Liki handle repetitive work, so practitioners can focus on professional judgment.»

Liki automates repetitive and systematic tasks, including:

- Chart generation
- True Solar Time calculation
- Calendrical computation
- Rule execution
- Knowledge organization
- Analytical assistance
- Report generation

Professionals remain responsible for:

- Professional judgment
- Practical experience
- Client communication
- Final decisions

Product Principles（产品原则）

1. Professional First

中文

始终按照命理师的专业标准设计产品。

English

Always design products to professional standards.

---

2. Workflow First

中文

围绕真实工作流，而不是围绕功能列表设计。

English

Design around real professional workflows instead of feature lists.

---

3. Deterministic Before AI

中文

先确定性计算，再 AI 推理。

命理引擎负责计算。

AI 负责理解、推理、组织与表达。

English

Deterministic computation comes before AI reasoning.

The Metaphysics Engine performs calculations.

AI focuses on understanding, reasoning, organization, and communication.

---

4. Verifiable

中文

所有计算与推导应尽可能可验证、可追溯。

English

Every calculation and conclusion should be as verifiable and traceable as possible.

---

5. Reduce Repetitive Work

中文

每一个功能，都应该帮助命理师减少重复劳动。

English

Every feature should reduce repetitive work for professionals.
