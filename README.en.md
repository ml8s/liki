## Why Liki

Ask a general-purpose LLM about the same chart three times and you may get three different answers. Chinese metaphysics amplifies small errors: miss a solar-term boundary by a day and the year pillar changes — every reading after it shifts.

So Liki turned each error-prone step into an explicit process: chart casting cross-checked against multiple implementations, readings emitted from rule truth tables, birth time normalized and calibrated before any judgment. **Conclusions are computed, not improvised.**

- ✅ **Reproducible charts** — same input, same chart, every time; solar terms and true solar time are handled by the astronomical engine
- ✅ **Traceable readings** — every conclusion cites its factor evidence, all the way to the classics (《三命通会》《滴天髓》…)
- ✅ **Honest boundaries** — time-sensitive charts get calibrated; insufficient evidence is labeled; nothing is silently defaulted

## Three deep dives

Three places most tools stay vague — and where Liki differs the most.

### 1｜The chart engine was cross-checked against three open-source implementations

The engine was not written once and shipped. `192` solar-term boundary cases (2024–2027, 24 terms per year × 2 anchors per term) are cross-checked with **lunar-python, sxtwl, and bazi-calculator** under a single policy: `all_oracles_must_agree` — one mismatch and the gate fails. The Ziwei implementation aligns with iztro-compatible data.

Where the engine disagreed with the classics, it was corrected against the original text and pinned as regression anchors:

> 《穷通宝鉴》 correction example: **乙木向阳，专取丙火；水多以戊佐。**
> The engine output matches this wording exactly, alongside full alignment with a 120-entry classical reference table.

### 2｜Direct readings from a truth table fitted with exam questions and classics

Readings are not generated by the model — they are **hit** from a truth table: **829 assertions, 893 condition groups, 1,355 condition rows**, driven by 511 natal factors and 111 annual factors. A hit yields a direct conclusion with its sources; a miss is reported as a miss. There is no room for improvised wording. Every assertion carries its evidence and classical source — for example, this real row:

| Assertion | Basis |
| --- | --- |
| Five elements lack Wood: liver / gallbladder / sinews / eyes run weak | Wood governs the liver (《黄帝内经》 "East generates wind; wind generates wood; wood generates sour; sour generates the liver") |

Two design details:

- Annual assertions **must carry year-activated factors**, or data validation fails — preventing natal traits from being repeated every year;
- **160 professional competition questions** (Mingli160) are used for rule-chain coverage and zero-hit checks, as a manual calibration/evaluation set.

### 3｜Time calibration — even when you hold a birth certificate

The most error-prone step in chart casting is "determining the time":

- Hospital clocks record standard time, not the sun at your birth moment — depending on longitude, true solar time can drift by half a shichen (double-hour);
- Born near a solar-term boundary, a few minutes change the year pillar;
- After 23:00, the "late Zi" rule makes the day pillar assignment error-prone.

So Liki makes calibration an explicit process instead of silently casting your chart from wall-clock time:

```text
your time + birthplace → normalization → candidate set (each with reasons)
→ full chart per candidate → hour-pillar-sensitive facts (pillar / ten gods / shen sha)
→ compare against your real events (2-3 candidates, 3-5 events with years) → calibrated result + confidence
→ insufficient evidence? labeled as such — never silently defaulted
```

### Engineering and privacy facts

- Explicit dual-system review: Bazi and Ziwei are computed separately; conflicts are listed by evidence layer.
- Skill and engine versions are CalVer-bound; old engines are rejected (fail-closed).
- Engine images are built and deployed by immutable digest (`ghcr.io/ml8s/liki-engine@sha256:...`).
- Sensitive questions go through a support-first safety policy.
- If a city is absent from the built-in table, `city_coords` may send the city name to Nominatim (OSM). Self-hosted deployments can disable this with `LIKI_EXTERNAL_GEOCODING=off`.

## FAQ

### What if I don't know the exact birth hour?

The Skill asks follow-up questions or uses real events for calibration. Insufficient evidence is labeled "insufficient calibration evidence"; the hour is never silently defaulted. Naming skips calibration and uses an expected-element strategy; babies and teenagers are read directly from the provided hour.

### I know my exact birth time — why calibrate?

Clock time is not destiny time: standard time (say, Beijing time) can drift from your birthplace's true solar time by half a shichen; a few minutes near a solar-term boundary changes the year pillar; the late-Zi rule makes the day pillar error-prone. These errors hide at boundaries you cannot see — so even with an accurate clock time, Liki normalizes first: list candidates with reasons, cast each, then calibrate against your real events. It is the first step of chart correctness.

### Does it need internet?

Yes. The Skill talks to the hosted `engine-mcp` (charts) and `counsel-mcp` (readings) MCP endpoints on liki.hk — install and ask, no configuration needed.

### Is my birth data stored?

No. Birth data remains in the current conversation context. It is not written to a local profile or submitted through feedback.

### How do I update?

Run `npx skills add https://liki.hk -y` when prompted. The Skill fails closed instead of calling stale RPCs.
